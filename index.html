How verdicts work. For each menu we match every prix-fixe dish to the restaurant's regular menu (exact name, or ≈ a clearly-same dish adjudicated under strict rules — same protein and preparation). Matched dishes give each menu a best-case combo (priciest matched pick per course) and worst-case combo (cheapest). Worth it however you order = even the worst combo beats the prix-fixe price. Order right = only some combos win. Skip = even the best combo loses. Dishes not on the regular menu are shown as "not priced" and excluded — never estimated. Menus with too little price data are labeled unpriced, not scored. Delivery-platform-sourced prices (flagged) run ~9–10% above dine-in, so those menus never earn the top badge. Distances are approximate (zip-code centers). Every restaurant links its price source and date.
